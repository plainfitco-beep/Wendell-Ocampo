About

Designer who renders,
renderer who designs

I'm Wendell Ocampo, a Creative Design Manager and 3D visualization specialist based in New Westminster, BC — currently the sole creative lead at Vinci Lighting, and an architectural 3D rendering specialist for Ironclad Development since 2018.

For 16+ years I've moved between architecture, 3D artistry and brand design: building accurate models from survey data and architectural plans, then lighting, texturing and rendering them into visuals that win clients and pre-sell developments.

My toolkit spans D5 Render, 3ds Max, V-Ray, Lumion, Unreal Engine and SketchUp — plus the Adobe Creative Suite for the retouching, layout and brand work that surrounds every render.
